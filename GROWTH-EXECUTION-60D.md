# Business Calculator Hub — 60-Day Growth Execution

## CEO objective
Build a sponsor-ready audience by concentrating acquisition on freelancers, consultants and small service businesses in English and Brazilian Portuguese.

## Non-negotiable rule
No new feature, market, affiliate or page unless it contributes directly to acquisition, retention or revenue in the next 60 days.

## North-star outcome
Create a repeatable path:
1. high-intent search/query
2. calculator or decision page
3. useful result
4. next business decision
5. contextual partner click
6. sponsor-ready audience evidence

## Phase 1 — Days 1–7
- Make Freelance Rate Calculator the primary acquisition product.
- Reposition global homepage around pricing/income decisions.
- Add copy/share result actions and GA4 share_result events.
- Prioritize the 50-query EN + PT-BR backlog in /api/growth.
- Pause expansion into additional markets until the focus cluster shows traction.

## Phase 2 — Days 8–30
Ship missing high-intent pages in this order:
1. consulting rate calculator
2. contractor hourly rate calculator
3. self-employed hourly rate calculator
4. freelance developer hourly rate
5. freelance designer hourly rate
6. freelance marketer hourly rate
7. freelance copywriter hourly rate
8. retainer pricing calculator
9. quanto cobrar por hora freelancer
10. quanto cobrar por projeto freelancer
11. quanto cobrar por consultoria
12. CLT ou PJ 5k / 8k / 10k / 15k cluster

Each new page must:
- answer one specific query
- link to the relevant calculator
- include self-canonical
- include schema required by QA
- include 3+ internal links
- include one contextual commercial next step
- avoid unverifiable legal/tax claims

## Phase 3 — Distribution
Daily operating loop:
- identify real questions in relevant communities
- answer with useful standalone information
- link only when a Hub tool genuinely improves the answer
- publish one short LinkedIn insight tied to a real pricing question
- record landing-page and outbound-click performance

No spam and no bulk link dropping.

## Phase 4 — Sponsor proof
Sponsor deck should lead with:
- audience: freelancers / consultants / small service businesses
- top decision pages
- search intent
- outbound partner CTR
- top markets
- repeat usage
- contextual inventory

Sell category sponsorship, not generic banners:
- Billing partner
- CRM partner
- Email & growth partner
- Payments partner
- Website/hosting partner
- Productivity partner

## Weekly scorecard
Track:
- organic users
- Google impressions
- clicks
- pages entering Top 20
- pages/session
- returning users
- share_result events
- affiliate_click events
- sponsor leads
- revenue

## Kill criteria
If the focused cluster shows no meaningful upward trend in impressions, clicks or users after 60 days of consistent shipping and distribution, change the acquisition thesis instead of adding more generic calculators.
